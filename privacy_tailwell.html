Tailwell Privacy Policy

Tailwell: Pet Health Tracker · Developer: HA DANG · Effective date: October 24, 2026 · Contact: tuyennv92.business@gmail.com

🇬🇧 English

Tailwell: Pet Health Tracker (“Tailwell”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) helps you keep your pets’ vaccines, medications, weight and vet records in one place, get reminders, and share care with your family. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who processes it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Tailwell apps for iPhone, iPad and Android. Tailwell is developed by HA DANG, who is responsible for your personal data (the “data controller”) and can be reached at tuyennv92.business@gmail.com.

1. Information you give us

  • Account. The app first creates an anonymous account so your data can sync. If you create an account with an email address and password, we receive your email address. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us.
  • Pet profiles and records. What you enter about your pets: name, species, breed, sex, neutered status, birth date or estimated age, color, photo, microchip number, insurance provider and policy number, allergies, conditions, diet, target weight, and your vet’s name, clinic name, phone number and address; vaccinations, medications, doses given or skipped, weights, vet visits, symptoms, notes and expenses.
  • Photos and documents. Pet photos and files you attach, such as vaccine certificates or lab results. The app opens your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo or document.
  • Family sharing. When you invite someone, we create a one-time invite code. Members of a shared pet can see that pet’s records, the display names and roles of the other members, and who logged a dose or uploaded a file. If you have an email account, your display name is the part of your email address before “@”; a guest account has no display name.

2. Information collected automatically

  • App settings. Your app language, time zone and weight unit, so reminders and notifications arrive at the right time and in your language, and a push notification token for your device.
  • Purchases. Which product you bought, whether it is active, its expiry date and the store’s transaction ID or purchase token, so we can verify your Premium access with Apple or Google. We never receive your card or other payment details.
  • Diagnostics and usage. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics), app performance data (Firebase Performance Monitoring) and usage events (Google Analytics for Firebase), such as “pet created”, “dose logged” or “paywall viewed”, and whether you have Premium, how many pets you have and their species. Usage events are linked to a random app-instance ID and to your account ID and, where available, to your device’s advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking). This data does not include the content of your records, notes or documents.
  • App integrity. Firebase App Check uses Apple App Attest / DeviceCheck or Google Play Integrity to confirm that requests come from the genuine app.
  • Advertising identifiers. See section 4.
  • Account records. When your account was created and last used, your reminder preferences and, on Android, an anonymous account ID that Google Play links to your purchases. The app keeps a copy of your data on your device so it works offline, and our server logs record your account ID to help us fix problems.

We do not collect your precise (GPS) location, your contacts or your own (human) health data. Google’s services (Analytics and AdMob) may infer your approximate location, such as your country or city, from your IP address.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the app: store and sync your pets’ records across your devices, schedule reminders, share pets with the family members you choose, and create PDF health records and data exports when you ask.
  • To send notifications: medication, vaccine, refill and weigh-in reminders, and family updates (for example, when a family member logs a dose).
  • To provide Premium: verify purchases and subscriptions with Apple or Google and unlock features.
  • To keep the app working and secure: fix crashes, improve performance, prevent abuse (such as guessing invite codes), and understand which features are used.
  • To show ads to users without Premium (see section 4).

We do not sell your personal information, and we never use your pets’ records for advertising.

4. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; Premium removes them. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) and similar information to show and measure ads. On iOS, personalized ads are used only if you allow tracking in Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent with Google’s consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & legal → Privacy choices (ads). Without consent, ads are limited and not personalized. Elsewhere, ads on Android may be personalized unless you delete your advertising ID (on older Android versions, turn on “Opt out of Ads Personalization”) in your device settings. On iOS, you can change your tracking choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Service providers

We use these providers to run the app and share data with them only as needed for the purposes in section 3. Each of them is bound by data protection terms and provides the same or equal protection of your data as described in this policy. Their privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Analytics, Remote Config, App Check): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store and Google Play, which process payments and tell us the status of your purchases: apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy
  • This web page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

6. Where your data is stored and how it is protected

Your account and pet data are stored on Google Cloud servers in the United States. If you live elsewhere, your data is transferred to the United States; Google provides safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Access rules on our servers let only you and the family members you share a pet with read that pet’s records. No method of storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

7. How long we keep data

  • Your account and pet records are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Data export files are deleted from our servers within 30 hours; their download link expires after one hour.
  • Invite codes expire after 7 days and are then deleted.
  • Crash, performance and analytics data are kept by Google for the retention periods of those Firebase services.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Help & feedback → Delete account. This permanently deletes from our servers: your account; every pet you own, including pets you share with others, with all of its records, photos and documents; and your purchase records and data exports. For pets owned by someone else, you are removed from the pet and your name is removed from the doses you logged; the records, doses and files you added stay with that pet for its owner and members, without your name. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings. You can also ask us to delete your account without the app: email tuyennv92.business@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Tailwell account” from the email address you sign in with, or include that address. We will delete your account and data as described above within 30 days. A guest account that never signed in can only be deleted in the app, because we cannot otherwise identify it.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability: Settings → Help & feedback → Export my data gives you a copy of your account and pet data (JSON). You can also share a PDF health record of each pet.
  • Correction: edit or delete any record in the app.
  • Deletion: see section 8.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in the app’s Settings or in your device settings.
  • Ads and tracking: see section 4.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, the UK, Switzerland, California or Brazil), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw your consent. To use these rights, use the tools in the app or email us; we will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Under some US state laws (such as California’s), personalized advertising can count as “sharing” personal information: you can opt out by not allowing tracking on iOS, by deleting your advertising ID (or opting out of ads personalization) on Android, or by emailing us. Where the GDPR applies, we process your data to perform our contract with you (providing the app), for our legitimate interests (security, fixing crashes and improving the app), and with your consent (personalized ads and tracking).

10. Children

Tailwell is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Pet health information

Tailwell is a record-keeping and reminder tool. It does not give veterinary or medical advice; always follow your vet’s instructions.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date and, for significant changes, may also tell you in the app.

13. Contact

Tailwell is developed by HA DANG. For any privacy question or request: tuyennv92.business@gmail.com
